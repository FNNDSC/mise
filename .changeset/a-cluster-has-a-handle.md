argus: a cluster has a handle. Every shape's anchor in the UNIVERSE is drawn as a halo sized by the feeds it gathers; hover names the shape and its count, a click brings the cluster into view (the shape lit, the rest dimmed, the camera framing it), BACK or Esc returns; `universe cluster <feed>` from the console. A feed entered from a cluster climbs back to the cluster.
