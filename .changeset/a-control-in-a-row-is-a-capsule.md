A control in a row is a capsule that reads what it will do next, and the browser speaks the console's vocabulary. The PACS fold cell is an OPEN / CLOSE capsule drawn from the group's state; the browser's `..` and `.` rows wear UP and HERE capsules; a lone glyph is no longer a control anywhere.
