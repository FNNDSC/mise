A control in a row is a capsule that reads what it will do next, and the browser speaks the console's vocabulary. Every listing row keeps navigating apart from selecting: a control cell activates the row (OPEN / CLOSE folds a PACS study, OPEN enters a directory or shows a file, UP and HERE on the browser's lead rows) and nothing else, while the row's body selects it and puts its verbs in the frame — so a directory can now be moved, copied, removed or its feed shared without entering it. The browser's rows carry the console's Nerd Font icons and the console's colour per kind, read from tokens the console publishes and held equal to the console's own table by a test.
