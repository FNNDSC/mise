A census is drawn as smoothly as a shape, and blooms with the wave. Census members took an 80-face icosahedron, faceted on a 4K screen; the cut now scales with how many are drawn (980 faces up to 2,000, 320 up to 20,000, 80 past that). Each census stage blooms as the wave lands on it, as a lone node's lamp does (none past 400 stages). PULSE stands on by default; a feed view kept before reads the new default for pulse alone.
