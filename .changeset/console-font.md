The console now uses JetBrainsMono Nerd Font Mono: the prompt's segments and icons sit on the same grid as the text. Share Tech Mono had none of those glyphs and borrowed them from MesloLGS NF at another width; the new face holds every glyph the prompt, the listings and the scans use at one cell (measured), with MesloLGS NF kept behind it for the rest. It is bundled as WOFF2 with its OFL licence. This readies the console for the terminal it becomes (#1125).
