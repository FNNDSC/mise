After `chell auth login`, a bare `chell` or a `chell -c "…"` goes through that door with no flags. That is how `gh` reaches GitHub once logged in; `--no-door` asks for the local chell anyway, and a `user@url` target still names a CUBE directly. `-e` now holds through a door too: the line runs a segment at a time and stops at the first whose answer is an error. A token within a week of its death is said so on every call; one past it is refused with the words to run.
