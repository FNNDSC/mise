A new find command walks the namespace through the listing cache and prints the paths its expression selects. Tests are -name, -iname, -path, -type and -newermt, bounded by -maxdepth and -mindepth, printed with -print or -print0, and joined by -o, ! and parentheses; a tree already seen is walked without asking CUBE. An unforced /proc/jobs roster check runs at most once a minute, and REFRESH still forces one.
