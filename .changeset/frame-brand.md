A session over a backend other than ChRIS no longer wears ChRIS's brand (#985). The header logo, the credit line's ChRIS clause, the brain splash, the favicon and the ChRIS packages in the version stack are the composition's; any other backend gets an empty splash, one console line naming the backend and a neutral favicon. A ChRIS session is unchanged.
