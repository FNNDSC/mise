sl, cmatrix, rain, asciiquarium, tetris and snake play in the terminal they are typed in; --pane draws them in ARGUS's GAMES pane. The programs move to `@fnndsc/menu/games` so chell and ARGUS run the same ones; chell takes the screen (alternate screen, raw keys, the console's hues) and gives it back on q, Esc or Ctrl-C, and marks the console `playing` meanwhile. The transcript console, with no screen to play on, keeps the pane.
