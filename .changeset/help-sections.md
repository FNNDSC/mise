Help is now a short table of contents instead of one long list. `help` shows each section (Navigation, Files, PACS, Workflows, Shell…) with what it is for, its size and a few commands, and `help PACS` lists that section's commands. The shell's features have their own pages (`help pipes`, `help handles`, `help pronouns`). New: `apropos <word>` (also `man -k`) finds commands by a word in their name or description, and `man <command>` shows a command's page.
