The keyboard now reaches every pane's frame and every listing's rows: PACS search to series needs no mouse. Over a listing, ↑↓ move a row cursor, Enter indicates a row and Enter again opens it; → or Ctrl-B r takes the keys into the frame, where ↑↓ or Tab walk the verbs and ← leaves. Esc in a pane's text field hands the keys back, a prefix focus move takes them out of the console, and Ctrl-B & (or `pane close all`) closes every pane.
