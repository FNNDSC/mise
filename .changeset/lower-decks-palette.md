LOWER DECKS has its palette again. It is the scheme selected by removing `data-theme`, so its seven colours have to sit on the bare root; they used to arrive with the imported stylesheet, and when that stopped shipping the scheme rendered black — every block drawn, every colour resolving to nothing. The seven are seated, which also restores the four state hues derived from them, and the four named schemes are unchanged to the property.
