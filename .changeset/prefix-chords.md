Prefix chords: with a pane's drawer open (Ctrl-B), one key presses one of its capsules — tmux's `%` `"` `z` `x` `o` `:`, `h j k l` to split, `H J K L` to move the pane, `m` to arm MOVE, `u f v` the binding, `1 2 3` the claim, `?` the table — and the arrows focus the pane in that direction; Tab still walks the verbs. Each capsule's title names its key; `argus keys` prints the table.
