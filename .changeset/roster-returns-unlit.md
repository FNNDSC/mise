A run OPENed from the DAG listing and left by Esc comes back to a roster with nothing lit. The press's acknowledgement (the lit row and the dimmed rest) waited for the roster's own rows to settle it, but a run's answer is its graph taking the pane, so the row stayed lit, the rest stayed dimmed, and no press could clear it. The DAG pane now settles it when the graph lands, when the feed is refused, or when Esc backs out before the graph arrives.
