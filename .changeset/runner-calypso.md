A daemon can now be allowed to start jobs that change a cloud and bill, with --host-control tasks; bare and all never grant it. The tier is named on its own because it reaches beyond this host into money. The daemon's surface declares it to the engine, jobs left running by the last launch are picked up again after the surface is installed, and the launch says how many. On a non-loopback bind the guard names spending rather than a shell when `tasks` is the only tier.
