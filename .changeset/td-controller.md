In a stratus session the TD controller takes the screen: every control sends a line, and the engine asks on its strip. `domain` opens it. A pill per domain and NEW; CONDITION, cost, build and what changed since the last command in the header; OVERVIEW, SIZE, BUILD, CLUSTER, CHRIS and TEARDOWN as views; the billing question answered by YES · BILL or NO, teardown's by the name typed back; SIZE's levers redrawing fit and cost from `domain size`, COMMIT sending `domain new`. A DOMAINS row opens it on that domain. Under 1250 px the line runs as text.
