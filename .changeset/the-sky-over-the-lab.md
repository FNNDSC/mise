brasa: `weather [place] [-c|--celsius] [-d|--days N]` — the conditions now and a short forecast for a place, from Open-Meteo (no key, no account), geocoded by name; Boston when no place is given. A kernel command, so a TTY and ARGUS show the same report; the typed `sys.weather` model carries the numbers beneath it. Unknown flags refused by name; an unreachable service or an unknown place answers in one line.
