The trek bridge draws marks in place of the C's letters, and scans long range on arrival as a standing order. The short-range scan is one picture: each sector a pressable cell carrying a symbol for what it holds (a ship, a Klingon, a starbase, an inhabited system, a star, a black hole), the ship's mark turning to the course last committed and wearing a shield ring whose width and colour are the shield over its maximum (thin and dashed when down, the starbase's when docked), each Klingon wearing one of four marks that the engine's own lines carry from sector to sector, and facing the ship. The same symbols sit on the course wheels' rims, lead the target pills and fill a legend beside the C's glyphs, so one mark means one thing everywhere; the stars breathe slowly, out of step, which is the one motion with no engine line behind it and stops under reduced motion. The charts leave an unscanned quadrant empty, mark a supernova with the radiation trefoil, and the long-range scan shows the edge of the galaxy as such. The bridge now sends the engine's own `l` at the first `Command:` prompt and whenever the ship arrives in a new quadrant, while the long-range sensors work, and folds the scan's lines in the ship's log under one dim head. The arrow keys walk the scan; Enter presses a sector; the engine's bell is no longer a glyph in the log.
