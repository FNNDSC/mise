The trek bridge moves: each frame replays an engine line or a state change, and the log scrolls with the picture. Frames play in the lines' order. A torpedo is an orange-red photon with a white heart and flickering pulses that flies the track the engine printed, point by point, trailing a comet tail; a Klingon flies the sectors its `moves to` line names on a red track, or off the edge when it escapes, and the long-range cell it fled to blooms; a kill, a nova, a misfire and a supernova burst at their sectors after the node flare; a phaser beam joins the ship to the Klingon the hit line names, a Klingon's shot reaches the ship and flares the shield ring, which loses the absorbed share the line reports (and no beam is drawn when the line names no sector); a move lays a dashed track from where the ship was to where the state puts it, a pulse runs it and the ship slides with it, and a crossing runs the ship off the edge, dims and rescans the grid, blooms the long-range cell that becomes HERE and runs it in from the matching edge. A command pill, a view or Esc jumps the picture to its end, and reduced motion always does. Four cues join the bridge's voice, `nova`, `supernova`, `klingon-pass` and `shield`, for now played with the nearest models in the bank until their own are chosen.
