SHARE VIEW gives you the lines that put a colleague exactly where you are. The new header pill (and `desktop share` in the console) shows your current view as a short script starting from `desktop clear` (which keeps your own screen as a PANES card, then goes to the dashboard), and copies it. Pasted or typed into the ARGUS console, the script rebuilds the same panes, graph settings and selected job.
