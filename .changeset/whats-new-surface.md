A WHAT'S NEW block on the dashboard says what the installed release changed, and opens the notes as a pane. The NOTES pane lists one block per release and a row per change, the rest unfolding on a press, with LATEST/LAST 5/ALL and INTERNAL pills; when the daemon is out of date, the notice says in one line what the newer release brings.
