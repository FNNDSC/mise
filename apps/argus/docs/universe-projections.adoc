== Abstract

The UNIVERSE pane draws everything a ChRIS identity has ever run as one space: every feed a small molecule of the jobs it ran, the molecules arranged so that the arrangement itself says something — which feeds are the same pipeline, which tools run with which, what data the work began from, in what order the lab grew. This document describes that space mathematically: what is projected, the choices on the frame that select a projection, the forces and procedures behind each of the six layouts (GALAXY, SPOKES, CLUMPS, CONSTELLATIONS, DATA, ACCRETION), how the placed space is drawn (stars, spheres, a census of every job, halos, captions, the hand-off between them), how time is shown (REPLAY), and what each costs. Every formula is the one in the code, cited by file; every screenshot is the operator's own space (709 feeds, 2,997 drawn stages, 46 plugins, 37 pipeline shapes) on the lab's CUBE, the pane zoomed to the whole viewport.

Where the design decisions behind a layout were argued, they are recorded in link:../../../packages/orrery/docs/orrery.adoc[orrery.adoc] (the drawing library's design and journal); the surface's side is in link:aegis.adoc[aegis.adoc] and the pane's reference in link:components.adoc[components.adoc].

== What is projected

=== Feeds, jobs and groups

A feed stem:[f] is a tree (with the occasional join) of plugin instances — jobs. A large feed may run hundreds of jobs of one plugin side by side (a conversion fanned over a series), so drawing jobs one-to-one would give a space of 210,012 nodes for this identity, which is neither drawable in a force layout nor readable. The session therefore collapses each feed: jobs are grouped by plugin at each place in the pipeline, and a group stands for all of them.

.A feed as run and as drawn: fourteen parallel conversions become one group of fourteen, its hue shifted toward error by the share that failed.
image::collapse.svg[Collapse,1180]

A group stem:[g] carries its plugin stem:[p_g], its job count stem:[n_g], its errored count stem:[e_g], its status, and its parent group. The shape of a feed is the string of its groups, `parent:plugin` joined by `>` (`r:pl-dircopy>0:pl-dcm2niix>1:pl-mri-preview`); feeds with the same shape ran the same pipeline (`apps/argus/src/features/dag/universe.ts`).

=== Size and colour

With the JOBS scale a group's metric grows with the log of its jobs, and its radius with its metric against the largest in the space:

[latexmath]
++++
m_g = \log_2(n_g + 1), \qquad r_g = r_0\left(\tfrac12 + 1.2\,\frac{m_g}{\max(1, \max_h m_h)}\right), \qquad r_0 = 0.55
++++

so radii run from stem:[0.275] (one job, in a space of giants) to stem:[0.935]. With the ALIKE scale every metric is 1 and every sphere the same (`packages/orrery/src/layout/molecule.ts`).

A group's colour blends from the palette's _done_ to its _error_ by the square root of the share of its jobs that failed, so that one failure in eighty thousand is visible but not alarming, and a group that failed outright is plainly red:

[latexmath]
++++
s_g = \min\!\left(1, \frac{e_g}{n_g}\right), \qquad c_g = c_{\text{done}} + \sqrt{s_g}\,\bigl(c_{\text{error}} - c_{\text{done}}\bigr)
++++

A group with any failure also carries an _ember_ — a solid core under its glow (`apps/argus/src/scene/chrisLook.ts`, `packages/orrery/src/draw/palette.ts`).

=== From the index to pixels

.The projection pipeline. The frame's choices select the graph; the layout engine is pure and runs in a worker; positions are kept per layout and held when a feed lands.
image::pipeline.svg[Pipeline,1300]

The kernel's `/proc` index (cumin's ProcCache, filled by salsa's sweeps) holds every feed's topology and, since slice 7 of the ORRERY epic, what each feed's data is. `proc universe` answers the collapsed feeds. ARGUS builds a scene graph for the chosen view and layout; the orrery layout engine places it (a pure function, run in a Web Worker so the page never stalls); the orrery drawing layer draws it.

== The frame's choices

A projection is the product of independent choices, each a block on the pane's mode frame and a word on the console (`universe <verb>`), all but the physics kept per identity.

[cols="1,2,3",options="header"]
|===
| Block | Values | What it changes

| VIEW | FEEDS · SHAPES | every feed its own molecule, or each pipeline shape folded into one molecule
| SCALE | JOBS · ALIKE | a sphere sized by its jobs, or every sphere alike
| DENSITY | SHAPE · CENSUS | a group as one sphere with its count, or every job its own point
| LAYOUT | GALAXY · SPOKES · CLUMPS · CONSTELLATIONS · DATA · ACCRETION | where every molecule stands
| DRAW | STARS · SPHERES | points of light far off, or lit spheres everywhere
| 3D / 2D | 3D · 2D | the space in depth, or flattened onto the screen
| CAPTIONS | ON · OFF | hub names over the picture
| REPLAY | play · pause · at a date | the space's history, feed by feed
|===

CONSTELLATIONS, DATA and ACCRETION each bring a graph of their own and need every feed, so they are offered in the FEEDS view only; SHAPES gives way to GALAXY.

== The common physics

Every settling layout is a velocity-Verlet simulation (d3-force-3d) of bodies stem:[i] with positions stem:[\mathbf{x}_i] and velocities stem:[\mathbf{v}_i]. Each tick applies the forces, scaled by a cooling parameter stem:[\alpha], then

[latexmath]
++++
\mathbf{v}_i \leftarrow (1 - \lambda)\,\mathbf{v}_i, \qquad \mathbf{x}_i \leftarrow \mathbf{x}_i + \mathbf{v}_i, \qquad \alpha \leftarrow \alpha + (\alpha_{\min} - \alpha)\,\delta
++++

with velocity decay stem:[\lambda = 0.4] and alpha decay stem:[\delta = 1 - 0.001^{1/300} \approx 0.0228]. The forces the layouts use:

Charge (repulsion, Barnes–Hut):: stem:[\Delta\mathbf{v}_i = \alpha \sum_j q_j\, \frac{\mathbf{x}_j - \mathbf{x}_i}{\lVert \mathbf{x}_j - \mathbf{x}_i \rVert^2}] with stem:[q_j = -6\,(r_j / r_0)^2] — a body repels as its area — cut off beyond a _reach_ when one is set.
Link (spring):: each edge stem:[(s,t)] is pulled to rest length stem:[L_{st} = r_s + r_t + 1.4]: with stem:[\ell = \lVert \mathbf{x}_t - \mathbf{x}_s \rVert], the ends move by stem:[\alpha\,k\,(\ell - L)/\ell] of their separation, split by degree; stiffness stem:[k = 1/\min(\deg s, \deg t)] unless a layout sets it.
Collide:: overlapping bodies are pushed apart until their centres are stem:[1.2\,(r_i + r_j)] apart (1.05 in CLUMPS).
Gravity (mass-weighted centring):: stem:[\Delta\mathbf{v}_i = -\alpha\,\gamma_i\,\mathbf{x}_i] with stem:[\gamma_i = 0.08\,(r_i/r_0)^2]: heavy bodies are held nearer the centre, so clusters gather rather than drift. On by default in the universe (GRAVITY on the frame).

The bound on reach matters for small spaces: with fewer than 240 nodes the universe sets a reach of 12, so a lone molecule hugs itself instead of spreading as far as its charge carries; a crowd is left unbounded (`apps/argus/src/features/universe/panel.ts`).

Chance in every layout is seeded: an FNV-1a hash of a molecule's key seeds a linear congruential generator, so the same lab draws the same space on every device and every visit, and a molecule arriving later turns no other (`packages/orrery/src/layout/seeded.ts`).

== The layouts

=== Molecules and their hubs

In GALAXY, SPOKES and CLUMPS a feed is a molecule hung from an unseen anchor — a _ghost_ hub, one per pipeline shape — so that feeds of one pipeline gather. Each molecule is first laid out alone (a small settle, charge reach 12, 60 ticks, re-centred), and the result is cached by the molecule's signature (its parent structure and radii): the next feed with the same signature reuses the shape, turned by a seeded yaw stem:[\psi \in [0, 2\pi)] and pitch stem:[\theta \in [-\pi/2, \pi/2)], so that like feeds are alike but not clones. A molecule then moves as one rigid body of radius stem:[\max(r_0, \max_i \lVert \mathbf{o}_i \rVert + r_i)] over its offsets stem:[\mathbf{o}_i] (`packages/orrery/src/layout/hierarchy.ts`).

=== SPOKES

Every feed a spoke from its shape's hub, its chain pointing out.

.SPOKES geometry, computed from the code's own formulas: directions from a Fibonacci shell, reach staggered by the golden ratio so neighbouring spokes do not collide.
image::spokes.svg[Spokes,1180]

For a hub of radius stem:[r_h] with stem:[n] feeds of mean body radius stem:[\bar r], the shell and the stem:[k]-th feed's distance are

[latexmath]
++++
S = \max\!\left(r_h + 2,\ \sqrt{n}\,\bar r \cdot 1.6\right), \qquad d_k = r_h + 1.4 + S\left(0.35 + 0.65\,\{k\varphi^{-1}\}\right) + \lVert \mathbf{o}_{\text{root}} \rVert
++++

where stem:[\{\cdot\}] is the fractional part and stem:[\varphi^{-1} \approx 0.618]. The direction is the stem:[k]-th point of a Fibonacci shell of stem:[n] points,

[latexmath]
++++
y_k = 1 - \frac{2(k + \frac12)}{n}, \qquad \phi_k = k\,\pi(3 - \sqrt5), \qquad \hat{\mathbf{u}}_k = \bigl(\cos\phi_k \sqrt{1 - y_k^2},\ y_k,\ \sin\phi_k \sqrt{1 - y_k^2}\bigr)
++++

and the molecule is turned (Rodrigues' rotation) so that the axis from its root to its centre points along stem:[\hat{\mathbf{u}}_k]: the root faces the hub. Hubs themselves repel (stem:[q = -1.5\,r_h]) and collide, over 60 ticks.

.SPOKES on the operator's space.
image::spokes.jpg[SPOKES,1600]

=== CLUMPS

The same molecules, packed round their hub by a settle of the rigid bodies: links at stem:[L = r_s + r_t + 1.4], charge stem:[-6(r/r_0)^2], collide stem:[1.05\,r], and gravity stem:[\min(1, 0.08(r/r_0)^2)] per axis, 60 ticks.

.CLUMPS.
image::clumps.jpg[CLUMPS,1600]

=== GALAXY (the default)

The space finding its own shape. GALAXY starts from the SPOKES arrangement, so that it refines rather than unfolds, then lets every node go: charge stem:[-6(r/r_0)^2], springs along every edge (a feed's root to its shape's ghost included) at stem:[r_s + r_t + 1.4], collide stem:[1.2\,r], mass-weighted gravity stem:[0.08(r/r_0)^2], 80 ticks from stem:[\alpha = 0.6] (`packages/orrery/src/layout/galaxy.ts`). The result is a few large clusters — here the three pipeline families this identity runs, the largest (a CIVET pipeline over four hundred scans) a sphere of radial molecules.

.GALAXY.
image::galaxy.jpg[GALAXY,1600]

=== CONSTELLATIONS

Plugins are stars; a feed lies between the stars it ran. The sky is laid out first, alone, as a map of which tools run with which; then the stars are held and every feed settles against them.

.CONSTELLATIONS in two phases.
image::constellations.svg[Constellations,1240]

How much a plugin _says_ about a feed is its inverse document frequency; a plugin in more than nine feeds in ten is a landmark and says nothing:

[latexmath]
++++
w_p = \begin{cases} \ln\dfrac{N}{n_p} & n_p / N \le 0.9 \\[4pt] 0 & \text{otherwise} \end{cases}
++++

with stem:[N] feeds and stem:[n_p] of them running stem:[p]. Two plugins' co-occurrence is the share of the rarer's feeds that ran both, stem:[\sigma_{ab} = c_{ab} / \min(n_a, n_b)]. Phase one settles the stars with springs of length and stiffness

[latexmath]
++++
L_{ab} = R\,(1.1 - \sigma_{ab}), \qquad k_{ab} = 0.05 + 0.6\,\sigma_{ab}, \qquad R = \max\!\left(6,\ 4\sqrt{S},\ 1.6\sqrt{M}\right)
++++

(stem:[S] stars, stem:[M] stages: the sky grows with what settles in it), charge stem:[-30] out to stem:[3R], collide stem:[3r], 160 ticks. Phase two holds the stars and springs each stage stem:[g] of feed stem:[f] to its own plugin's star with

[latexmath]
++++
k_g = \frac{0.3 + 0.6\, w_{p_g} / \max_p w_p}{\sqrt{\lvert P_f \rvert}}, \qquad L_g = r_{\star} + r_g + 1
++++

where stem:[P_f] is the set of distinct plugins the feed ran: the rarest plugin pulls hardest, and a long pipeline is not dragged harder than a short one. Stages keep their own edges (stiffness 1) and repel within a reach of 5, 120 ticks. Tuned against this space offline: the median distance from a stage to its own star fell from 172 to 13 (`packages/orrery/src/layout/constellations.ts`).

.CONSTELLATIONS: the ringed points are plugin stars.
image::constellations.jpg[CONSTELLATIONS,1600]

A press on a star lights the stages that ran it across the whole sky and names the plugin, its feeds and its errored share:

=== DATA

Every feed hung from what it began from: a hub per format, under DICOM a hub per modality, under that a hub per series-description group.

.The DATA hub tree (counts are this space's).
image::data.svg[DATA,1180]

What each feed's data is — its format, and for DICOM its modality and series description read from one header prefix — is read once by the kernel and kept in the index (`packages/salsa/src/dag/feedData.ts`). Descriptions are compared by their words, not their spelling. A description becomes a word set stem:[W(d)]: upper case, separators to spaces, and words that say nothing about the sequence dropped (pure numbers, matrix sizes `256X256`, `FOV220`, `3MM`). Two word sets are one series when their overlap is at least a half,

[latexmath]
++++
O(A, B) = \frac{\lvert A \cap B \rvert}{\min(\lvert A \rvert, \lvert B \rvert)} \ \ge\ \tfrac12
++++

(over the smaller, so `SAG MPRAGE` is the same series as `SAG MPRAGE 220 FOV`). Groups form greedily, most common description first, each named by the description most of its feeds carry (`apps/argus/src/features/dag/dataSpace.ts`).

Hubs are massless ghosts drawn as nebulae of radius stem:[\rho(n) = 1.6 + 0.45\sqrt{n}] for the stem:[n] feeds beneath them, and the space settles as the galaxy does. A hub is captioned when it is a format or a modality, or when stem:[n \ge \max(5,\ 0.02\,N)].

.DATA, 708 of 709 feeds read: NIfTI · 435 (the CIVET inputs), DICOM · 234 (of which 203 are shared feeds whose files this identity may list but not read — a CUBE sharing gap — so NO MODALITY), and the rest.
image::data.jpg[DATA,1600]

=== ACCRETION

The space grown in the order it was made. Feeds arrive oldest first; the first stands at the centre; each after it walks in from a sphere round what has grown until it touches a feed already placed, and sticks there with a chance equal to how alike the two are. Like grows on like, the oldest work lies deepest, and a stranger starts a limb of its own. This is diffusion-limited aggregation biased by similarity (`packages/orrery/src/layout/accretion.ts`).

.One walker, simulated with the engine's rules: launched at random, drifting toward its kin, pushed off a stranger, sticking to its own family.
image::accretion.svg[Accretion,1180]

Likeness is the weighted overlap of the plugins two feeds ran, with the constellations' weights:

[latexmath]
++++
L(a, b) = \frac{\sum_{p \in P_a \cap P_b} w_p}{\sum_{p \in P_a \cup P_b} w_p}
++++

A feed of radius stem:[r] is launched at a seeded point on the sphere of radius stem:[R_L = E + 3r + r_{\max}] (stem:[E] the extent grown so far) and steps stem:[s = \max(0.5,\ r/2)] along

[latexmath]
++++
\mathbf{h} = (1 - \delta)\,\hat{\mathbf{w}} + \delta\,\frac{\mathbf{x}_k - \mathbf{x}}{\lVert \mathbf{x}_k - \mathbf{x} \rVert}, \qquad \delta = 0.35
++++

a random unit stem:[\hat{\mathbf{w}}] blended with a drift toward its _kin_ stem:[k], the placed feed most like it. It touches a placed feed stem:[j] when stem:[\lVert \mathbf{x} - \mathbf{x}_j \rVert < r + r_j + 0.4], and then sticks against the most alike of those it touches with probability

[latexmath]
++++
P(\text{stick}) = \max\bigl(0.01,\ L(f, j)\bigr)
++++

else it is pushed off and walks on. After 400 steps it takes a free place touching its kin. A walker that strays beyond stem:[2R_L] is launched again. Each feed is a compact tree: its root at its heart, each child one edge (stem:[r_{\text{parent}} + r_{\text{child}} + 1.4]) past its parent, its heading tilted by a seeded angle in stem:[[0, \pi/3)] off its parent's. Drift and floor were tuned on synthetic labs of three pipeline families: a feed grew beside its own family six times in seven. The first cut unfolded each feed with the DAG pane's settle — fourteen units wide — and the real space drew as spokes; the compact tree has a median radius of 3.6. Nothing already placed moves: a feed that lands later accretes as the newest, and `universe regrow` grows the coral afresh. For this space the growth takes 2.5 s in the worker.

.ACCRETION.

=== CENSUS: every job

Under CENSUS a group of stem:[n] jobs becomes stem:[n] points on a Fibonacci shell round its stage, of radius and member size

[latexmath]
++++
R_{\text{shell}} = r\,(1.6 + 0.55\, n^{1/3}), \qquad r_{\text{member}} = \max\!\left(0.06,\ \min\!\left(\tfrac{r}{2},\ \frac{2.2\,R_{\text{shell}}}{\sqrt n}\right)\right)
++++

and member stem:[k] of a group joins member stem:[k \bmod n_{\text{parent}}] of its parent's, so a chain of equal fans reads as parallel filaments (`packages/orrery/src/draw/censusField.ts`).

== Drawing

=== Stars

Far off, every node is a point of light — one draw call for the whole space. A star's size on screen is its sphere's projected diameter enlarged by 1.8 and clamped,

[latexmath]
++++
\text{px} = \operatorname{clamp}\!\left(1.8 \cdot \frac{2 r\, S}{z},\ 2,\ 28\right)(1 + 1.6\,\phi), \qquad S = \frac{H}{2\tan(\text{fov}/2)}
++++

with stem:[z] its depth, stem:[H] the view height in pixels and stem:[\phi] its replay flash; its glow is a smooth core with a halo falling as stem:[(1 - d)^{2.2}], drawn additively (`packages/orrery/src/draw/stars.ts`). A plugin star wears a ring. Ghost hubs are nebulae of radius stem:[\rho(n)]; threads between stars are faint lines.

=== Spheres, and the hand-off

Close up a molecule becomes lit spheres joined by tubes carrying pulses from parent to child. Which a molecule is drawn as is decided per molecule in screen pixels, with hysteresis so it does not flicker at the boundary:

.The hand-off between stars and spheres.
image::handoff.svg[Hand-off,1180]

[latexmath]
++++
\text{px}_{\text{mol}} = \frac{2\, r_{\max} S}{z_{\min}}, \qquad \text{target} = \begin{cases} \text{spheres} & \text{px}_{\text{mol}} > 7 \\ \text{stars} & \text{px}_{\text{mol}} < 5 \end{cases}, \qquad \mu \leftarrow \mu \pm \frac{\Delta t}{300\ \text{ms}}
++++

Spheres and tubes are drawn at opacity stem:[\mu], stars and threads at stem:[1 - \mu] (`packages/orrery/src/draw/handoff.ts`). SPHERES on the frame draws every node lit and solid regardless:

.GALAXY drawn as spheres.

=== Captions

A hub's name is drawn on a dark plate, the same size on screen at any distance (2.8% of the view's height). Where two captions meet on screen the one naming more feeds is shown and the other hidden until hovered — checked every frame, since captions are few. A ghost hub's caption stands at the centre of what it holds, not where its forces leave it (`packages/orrery/src/draw/labelField.ts`). CAPTIONS OFF hides them all.

=== 2D

The 2D projection flattens the placed space onto the screen: every node's depth is set to zero, spheres become discs, the idle spin stops, and a drag on empty space pans instead of orbiting. The worker's engines still settle in three dimensions; what is kept is unflattened, so returning to 3D loses nothing.

== Time: REPLAY

REPLAY plays the space's history: every feed hidden until the moment it was made, then shown where it stands now, with a flash. The replay clock advances

[latexmath]
++++
t \leftarrow t + \Delta t_{\text{wall}} \cdot \frac{t_{\text{last}} - t_{\text{first}}}{30\,000\ \text{ms}} \cdot v, \qquad \phi = 1 - \frac{t_{\text{wall}} - t_{\text{arrived}}}{900\ \text{ms}}
++++

so the whole history plays in thirty seconds at speed stem:[v = 1], and each arrival flashes for 0.9 s (`packages/orrery/src/controls/replay.ts`). It works over any layout. This identity made half its feeds in two days of January 2025:

== Descent

Entering a feed (a double press, or `universe enter <feed>`) replaces its molecule with its full graph — every job group, its tubes and lamps — settled alone with a reach of 12 and no gravity, the rest of the space dimmed but held in place behind it; BACK on the frame, or Esc, climbs out.

== Memory and cost

Each layout keeps its own positions per identity, in the browser and in the session: the first browser to settle a layout puts its places to the session (`proc layout put`), which keeps them beside the index checkpoint, and any browser that keeps none draws from the session's copy at once (8.1 s to a settled space for a clean browser before, 1.5 s after). When a feed lands, every standing node is held (frozen at its place) and only the newcomer settles — for ACCRETION, only the newcomer walks — so nothing the operator has seen moves. The engines run in a Web Worker behind a progress readout, so the page never stalls; measured for this space, ACCRETION grows in 2.5 s. A repaint that moves nothing — lighting a plugin, a hub, a caption toggle — settles nothing and draws in place.
